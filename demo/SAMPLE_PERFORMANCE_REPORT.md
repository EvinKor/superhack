Priority 2 high-impact issues were addressed in an average of 11.3 minutes, while Priority 3 standard requests received responses within 18.5 minutes on average. These response times position us in the top quartile of MSP performance benchmarks.

Resolution times also showed favorable trends, with an overall average of 3.9 hours from ticket creation to closure. Critical P1 incidents were resolved in an average of 2.1 hours, significantly better than our 4-hour SLA commitment. Standard P3 tickets averaged 4.2 hours to resolution, which, while slightly above our internal target of 4 hours, remains competitive given the complexity of issues handled. The month saw no SLA breaches on critical or high-priority incidents, maintaining our perfect compliance record for the quarter.
